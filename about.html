About Mahaveer Group

With over 27 years of excellence, Mahaveer Group has been shaping the future of urban living across South India through thoughtfully designed communities built on quality, trust, and innovation. Headquartered in Bengaluru with a strong presence in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, we have delivered 125+ residential and commercial developments, spanning over 26 million sq. ft. of meticulously planned spaces.

Today, 20,000+ families proudly call a Mahaveer home their own—a reflection of the trust we've earned through customer-centric design, uncompromising construction quality, and timely delivery. Our diverse portfolio includes premium apartments, luxury villas, plotted developments, integrated townships, and commercial spaces, thoughtfully crafted to meet the evolving needs of modern lifestyles.

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, Mahaveer Group upholds the highest standards of quality, sustainability, and ethical business practices. Guided by experienced leadership and driven by innovation, we continue to create future-ready communities that enrich lives, inspire confidence, and deliver lasting value for generations.

Driven by Purpose. Guided by Vision.

Every Mahaveer development is shaped by a clear vision and a steadfast mission—to create thoughtfully designed communities that inspire trust, enrich lives, and deliver lasting value for generations.

Vision

To redefine urban living by creating thoughtfully designed, premium yet affordable homes that enhance everyday lifestyles, foster a sense of belonging, and build strong, connected communities. Guided by sustainable practices and long-term thinking, the vision is to contribute meaningfully to economic growth while ensuring responsible development for future generations.

Mission

To craft exceptional homes with unwavering integrity, professional expertise, and cutting-edge technologies, delivering not just structures but thoughtfully designed living spaces that enhance lifestyles, foster community, and create lasting value. Through every project, we strive to set new benchmarks in quality, innovation, and sustainability, ensuring our customers experience trust, comfort, and pride in their homes.

Our Journey

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has been recognised by leading industry institutions.

1998–1999

Founding & Early Vision

Mahaveer Group was founded under the leadership of Praveen Reddy Kumbala during Bengaluru's IT boom, with a clear vision to create affordable, high-quality homes, supported by a strong commitment to thoughtful project planning.

2000–2001

Diversification & Growth

The Group expanded into apartments and plotted developments, leveraging engineering expertise from Reddy Structures to ensure durable, high-standard construction.

2002–2003

Land Bank Acquisition & Incorporation

Mahaveer acquired 100+ acres of land, with initial delivery by 2004. Formal incorporation enabled structured operations, regulatory compliance, and scalable growth.

A Vision That Continues to Inspire

Raveen Reddy Kumbala, Founder and Chairman of Mahaveer Group, established the company in 1997-98 with a vision to redefine urban living through quality construction, transparent practices, and customer-first values. A qualified civil engineer, he has led Mahaveer Group to become one of South India's most trusted real estate developers.

Under his leadership, the company has built a strong reputation for innovation, integrity, and sustainable development while maintaining the highest standards of quality and governance. His commitment to creating thoughtfully designed communities continues to shape Mahaveer's legacy of trust, excellence, and lasting value.
